How to Play 4D Tetris

What is 4D Tetris?

4DTRIS extends traditional Tetris into four spatial dimensions. Instead of pieces falling on a 2D plane, they fall through a 4D space (X, Y, Z, W axes) and are visualized as 3D projections.

Game Objective

Clear complete 3D "hyperplanes" by filling all positions at a given W-level. When a hyperplane is complete, it clears, and you earn points.

The 4D Space

The game board is a 2×2×2×2 hypercube (extending to 12 levels in the W direction). Think of it as multiple 3D layers stacked in the 4th dimension.

Pieces (Tetrominos)

There are 6 types of 4D pieces, ranging from 1 to 4 blocks:

  • Dot (1 block): Single hypercube
  • Line (2 blocks): Two connected hypercubes
  • Corner (3 blocks): L-shaped configuration
  • Tetracube (4 blocks): Various 4-block arrangements

4D Rotations

In 4D, there are 6 planes of rotation (compared to 1 in 2D Tetris):

  • XY, XZ, YZ: Traditional 3D rotations
  • XW, YW, ZW: Rotations involving the 4th dimension

Visualization

The 4D game space is projected into 3D for display. Different W-positions are shown with varying transparency and colors to help you visualize depth in the 4th dimension.

Tips for Success

  • Start slowly and understand how pieces move in 4D space
  • Pay attention to the W-axis (4th dimension) position
  • Experiment with different rotation planes
  • Try to clear multiple hyperplanes at once for bonus points
